# Code of conduct

Be technically rigorous and personally respectful. Critique code, evidence,
and claims—not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, disclosure of private information, and retaliation are not
accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to
protect a constructive project. Enforcement decisions should be proportional,
documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or unrelated personal data.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner, `kekePower`,
through GitHub. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private reporting path
described by [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not in conduct reports or public
issues.
